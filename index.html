Laura Pérez Molina

PhD Candidate in Particle Physics

Welcome to my website! I'm Laura, a PhD candidate in Particle Physics in the Neutrino Group at CIEMAT (Madrid, Spain). My research has been carried out within the frame of the international DUNE collaboration. In particular, our group was in charge of testing and validating DUNE Photon Detection System. I actively participated in these activities, specifically with the work developed at CERN Neutrino Platform (Switzerland) where I have been a visitor scientist.

More details on this can be found in my 📚 PhD thesis.
